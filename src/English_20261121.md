**English Corner:**

你的描述翻成自然的英文：

> "I ran into a bug. When I tried to delete this item, I got an error. It looks like it was actually deleted, but it's still showing on the screen."

- **「遇到 bug 了」**：`I ran into a bug` 或 `I hit a bug` 都很道地。`run into` 是「碰到、遇上」的意思。
- **「當我選擇刪除這項」**：不用直譯 `choose to delete`，用 `When I tried to delete this item` 更自然。
- **「殘留在畫面上」**：可以說 `still showing on the screen` 或 `still lingering in the UI`。工程師常說「畫面資料過期」是 `stale UI` 或 `stale state`。

**English Corner:**

你的句子翻成自然的英文：

> "Doesn't seem like it, but I tested it after your fix and it works. Please go ahead and deploy it."

- **「似乎都沒有」**：回答前一個問題時，口語會說 `Doesn't seem like it` 或 `Not that I can tell`，比直譯 `It seems there is none` 自然很多。
- **「測試 ok 了」**：可以說 `it works`、`it checks out`，或 `it passed testing`。
- **「請上線吧」**：`Please go ahead and deploy it` 或 `Go ahead and ship it`。`go ahead and…` 帶有「放心去做吧」的授權語氣。
