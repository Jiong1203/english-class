**English Corner:**

你的句子: *「我的最後進度是 "ff6ffd2..."，後面有有多了好幾版，請問後面的那幾版是做了甚麼?」*

中文裡有個小筆誤「有有多了」,自然的英文說法可以是:

| 不自然 | 自然 |
| --- | --- |
| My last progress is ff6ffd2, after that has has several versions, what did those do? | My last checkpoint was `ff6ffd2` — there are several commits on top of it now. What do those commits do? |

**重點**:
- 用 **checkpoint** 或 **last commit I saw** 比 "last progress" 自然
- **on top of it** 是 git 圈常用的說法,表示「在這之上又疊了哪些 commit」
- 詢問「做了什麼」用 **What do those commits do?** 比直譯的 "what did they do" 更貼近 review 情境(commit 是現在式存在的東西)
